# Third-Party Risk Workflow

```text
Business need identified
        ↓
Vendor intake and service classification
        ↓
Initial risk tiering
        ↓
Security / compliance due diligence
        ↓
Questionnaire and evidence review
        ↓
Gap identification and follow-up
        ↓
Contract / control requirements
        ↓
Risk decision / approval
        ↓
Onboarding
        ↓
Periodic review / trigger-based reassessment
```

## Example review dimensions

- Data handled and sensitivity
- Service criticality
- Access level
- Hosting / processing model
- Security certifications and assurance reports
- Business continuity / resilience
- Sub-processors / fourth parties
- Contractual security obligations
- Open findings and remediation commitments

## Governance model

| Stage | Primary owner | Typical support |
|---|---|---|
| Business need | Business owner | Procurement |
| Risk tier | Vendor Risk / Security | Business owner |
| Due diligence | Security / Compliance | Legal, Privacy, Procurement |
| Remediation | Vendor + control owner | Project / Vendor manager |
| Risk acceptance | Accountable business / risk owner | Security / Compliance |
| Periodic review | Vendor owner | Vendor Risk |

## Programme-management focus

The key delivery challenge is not the questionnaire itself; it is ensuring that findings have owners, material gaps are resolved or accepted, dependencies are visible and onboarding does not proceed without required decisions.

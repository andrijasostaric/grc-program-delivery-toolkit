## Evidence quality checks

Evidence should be:
- **Relevant** — directly demonstrates the control operating.
- **Current** — covers the required audit period.
- **Traceable** — source and owner are known.
- **Complete** — not missing key approvals, dates or populations.
- **Repeatable** — the organisation can produce equivalent evidence again.

## Programme rule

A control is not treated as audit-ready simply because a policy exists. Readiness requires evidence that the relevant process or control is actually operating where applicable.

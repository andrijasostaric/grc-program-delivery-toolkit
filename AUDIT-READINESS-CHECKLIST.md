# Audit Readiness Checklist

## Scope and governance
- [ ] ISMS / programme scope is approved and understood.
- [ ] Key stakeholders and control owners are identified.
- [ ] Governance cadence and escalation path are active.

## Documentation
- [ ] Required policies and procedures are approved and current.
- [ ] Document owners and review dates are defined.
- [ ] Procedures reflect how teams actually operate.

## Risk and controls
- [ ] Risk register is current.
- [ ] Treatment actions have owners and dates.
- [ ] Control ownership is explicit.
- [ ] High-impact gaps are closed or formally accepted.

## Evidence
- [ ] Required evidence sources are mapped.
- [ ] Samples are current and traceable.
- [ ] Missing evidence is tracked as remediation.
- [ ] Teams know how to retrieve evidence during audit.

## People and operating readiness
- [ ] Interview participants understand their responsibilities.
- [ ] Relevant training is complete.
- [ ] Key processes have operated for a sufficient period to produce evidence.
- [ ] Open actions from exercises / reviews are tracked.

## External audit preparation
- [ ] Audit timetable and participants confirmed.
- [ ] Evidence index prepared.
- [ ] Known gaps and responses agreed.
- [ ] Internal readiness review completed.
- [ ] Leadership has reviewed residual risks.

## Go / no-go question

**Can the organisation consistently explain who owns each material control, how it operates, and produce evidence that supports that explanation?**

# ISO 27001 Readiness Programme Plan

## Objective
Prepare a fictional SaaS organisation for an external ISO 27001 certification audit by establishing clear scope, control ownership, evidence readiness and remediation governance.

## Workstreams

| Workstream | Focus | Example outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Scope & governance | ISMS scope, stakeholders, cadence | Scope statement, governance model |
| Policies & procedures | Required documented practices | Policy set, operating procedures |
| Risk management | Identify, assess and treat risks | Risk register, treatment plan |
| Control ownership | Assign accountable owners | Control-owner matrix |
| Evidence readiness | Collect and validate operating evidence | Evidence tracker |
| Remediation | Close control and documentation gaps | Action plan, due dates, owners |
| Internal readiness | Test before external audit | Readiness review, mock evidence checks |
| Audit coordination | Manage external audit preparation | Audit schedule, evidence pack, issue log |

## Example milestones

1. Scope and governance agreed
2. Control owners confirmed
3. Baseline gap assessment completed
4. Priority remediation plan approved
5. Evidence collection operational
6. Internal readiness review complete
7. Critical gaps closed
8. External audit preparation complete

## Governance

- Weekly delivery review for owners and remediation.
- Bi-weekly leadership update for readiness, risks and decisions.
- Dedicated escalation for overdue high-impact gaps or missing evidence.
- Readiness decision before external audit based on objective completion criteria.

## Success criteria

- All in-scope controls have accountable owners.
- Required evidence is current, traceable and accessible.
- High-impact remediation items are closed or formally risk-accepted.
- Audit participants understand responsibilities and evidence sources.
- No material audit-readiness issue is first discovered during the external audit.

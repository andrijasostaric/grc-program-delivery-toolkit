# Control Owner Matrix

| Control area | Accountable owner | Supporting teams | Delivery focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access control | Head of IT / Security | Engineering, HR | Joiner/mover/leaver, privileged access, reviews |
| Incident management | Security Lead | Engineering, Operations, Legal | Response process, roles, testing, evidence |
| Business continuity | Operations Lead | IT, Security, People | BCP, recovery roles, exercises |
| Supplier security | Procurement / Risk | Security, Legal, Finance | Due diligence, contracts, periodic review |
| Change management | Engineering Lead | Product, Security | Approval, testing, release evidence |
| Logging & monitoring | Security / Platform Lead | Engineering | Logging coverage, alert ownership, retention |
| Risk management | Risk / Security Lead | Business owners | Assessment, treatment, acceptance |
| Security awareness | People / Security | All teams | Training completion and follow-up |

## Ownership rule

The programme manager coordinates delivery but does not become the control owner. Accountability should stay with the function that operates and can sustainably maintain the control after certification.

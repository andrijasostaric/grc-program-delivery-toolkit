## Prioritisation

Remediation should be prioritised according to:
1. control / business impact;
2. audit timing;
3. dependency on other workstreams;
4. effort and lead time;
5. whether a sustainable operating process exists after the fix.

## Escalation

High-impact items that are overdue, blocked, unowned or likely to remain open at readiness review should be escalated early with a clear recommendation and decision request.
